Privacy Policy

VEYRO

Veyro works without an account, and everything it saves stays on your phone.

No accounts, no servers of our own

There is no sign-up, and Veyro has no server of its own. The whole game works offline.

What is saved, and where

Your records, coins, upgrades, story progress, collection and settings are saved on this device only. You can delete all your progress yourself in Options → Privacy and data.

Google Play Games (optional)

On Android, if you use Google Play Games, Veyro uses it for achievements and leaderboards. Google manages your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), your scores and your achievements under its own privacy policy. Veyro never asks for your email or password. If you do not use Play Games, everything stays on your phone.

Permissions

Veyro asks for no sensitive permission: no camera, no microphone, no contacts, no location and no files. It uses vibration and, for the optional Google Play Games features, internet access.

Ads and purchases

There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Who it is for

Veyro is an arcade game with mild fantasy combat against sea creatures, and no inappropriate language or adult content. There is no chat and no way for one player to contact another; leaderboards only show your Play Games gamer name.

Contact

If you report a problem, Veyro opens your email app with the message already written: the app version, your phone model and your language. You read it and decide whether to send it; the game sends nothing by itself.
